Privacy Policy for Eventora Planner

Last Updated: 2026

1. Introduction

Eventora Planner ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed to help users create, manage, and receive reminders for events. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture (optional)

2.2 Event Data

  • Title
  • Date and time
  • Location
  • Description
  • Reminder settings

2.3 Device & Usage Data

  • Device type
  • App usage logs (for debugging and performance)

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide event creation and reminder features
  • Sync your data across devices
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Generate AI-assisted event suggestions

4. Third-Party Services

This app uses trusted third-party services:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase AI Logic (Gemini)

These services may process your data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is securely stored using Firebase services
  • Each user’s data is isolated and protected
  • We take reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized access

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties except:

  • When required for app functionality (Firebase services)
  • When required by law

7. User Control

  • You can edit or delete your events anytime
  • You can sign out to stop data syncing
  • You may request data deletion by contacting us

8. Notifications

The app may send local notifications for event reminders. These are generated on your device and are not used for tracking.

9. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
